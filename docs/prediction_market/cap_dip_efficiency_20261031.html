3. How to read “efficient”

  • Start cap: after-fee both-Yes closer to $1 leaves less door charge. Efficiency here = highest path EV / total without collapsing N. 1.04 wins on the wider books; 1.03 wins EV on the tiny tennis settled 3¢ door set; 1.05 is live HOLD and still the volume peak inside the live-B filter.
  • Dip: larger D means a cheaper favorite target and a fatter locked extra pair when it fills, but more crossed games never dip. Efficiency = best EV while dips still print. 40¢ is the EV max; 35¢ is the last rung with ~half the crosses still hitting; below 20¢ the book goes red; 0¢ is not efficient.
