Research only · vz_breakout_amt_time_20260813 · same rw126 DualPaul78 closed set (N=5313)

Prior to the rw63 trade-count cut. Amount = how far price went above the zone before the retest. Time = bars from upside break close to fill (bars_after_break, includes T+1 next-open).
You already measure both. Neither is a usable continuous ranking filter. Spearman vs PnL is ~0 for time and run-up, +0.025 for break-bar distance. Late retests (64–126d) are a weaker group — that is the rw63 cut already made. Tiny clears (<0.25%) look worse on means than ≥3% clears, but |r|≲0.04 so it is not a sort key.
