1. Hypothesis

Zone strength = volume on any day, after the zone is known and through the signal bar, where Open or Close sits inside the zone band [lo, hi]. Relative volume (day volume / 20-day SMA) is the primary cross-symbol metric — raw share volume is not comparable across names.

Question: does that strength correlate with trade PnL% or book Ann ROR?

Causal window: bars (zone.created_on_idx, signal_idx]. Origin max-vol day is reported separately and is not mixed into the overlap sum (that day already defined the zone).

7. How to read this

  • Useful filter: Spearman |r| ≳ 0.15 with a monotone quartile lift in PnL and book Ann ROR, holding on OOS.
  • Weak / none: |r| ≲ 0.10 and Q4 not clearly better than Q1 — overlap volume is not a ranking signal on this freeze.
  • Per-trade Ann ROR is noisy (short winners inflate it). Prefer book Ann ROR by quartile.
