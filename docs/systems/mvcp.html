MVCP — Minervini VCP

MVCP (Minervini Volatility Contraction Pattern) is a Specific Entry Point Analysis (SEPA) Stage-2 pivot-breakout sleeve. The engine is kept; the sleeve is parked from DailyRun and active $500k allocation as of 2026-08-12.

1. What it is

Template (Relative Strength percentile, 200-SMA rising, position vs 52-week high/low) plus Volatility Contraction Pattern geometry (2–6 contractions, shrinking depth, volume dry-up), then a volume-confirmed Stage-2 break of the pivot with limited extension. Not a BRT/YH/WPBR remap.

2. Entry logic

Defaults from MvcpConfig / run_minervini_vcp.bat (Theory + 40_engine_plan freeze):

  1. RS template: RS percentile ≥ mvcp_rs_min_percentile (prod/research default 80) on a 252-bar lookback vs the RS universe (mvcp_rs_universe=data_dir).
  2. Trend template: 200-SMA rising over 21 bars; close ≥ 30% above 52w low; close within 25% of 52w high.
  3. VCP geometry: 2–6 contractions, depth shrink ≤ 0.65, first depth ≤ 40%, final depth ≥ 2%, base 15–120 bars, optional volume dry-up (0.85) with soft confirm.
  4. Prior advance: +20% in 63 bars (required by default).
  5. Stage-2 break: volume ≥ 1.5×, extension above pivot ≤ 5%.
  6. Fill: host next-open path. Reentry cooldown 20 days. Peer systems neutralized (brt/yh/wpbr/rl/rs off, indicator_buy=off).

3. Exit logic

  • Stop: stop_pct=0.92 multiplier (env MVCP_STOP).
  • Target: target_pct=1.25 (env MVCP_TARGET).
  • Time stop: 10 bars (mvcp_time_stop_bars), with a min-gain qualifier in config (5%).
  • Trail: arm at +10% (mvcp_trail_arm_pct), 20-SMA trail.
Time stop and SMA trail are not broker resting orders — handle manually if you ever re-enable the sleeve.
