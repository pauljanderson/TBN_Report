RS — Relative Strength

RS (Relative Strength) buys names beating SPY on 1Y/2Y/3Y excess returns that also show Strong Intermediate/Short/Long Trend Condition (TC) outlooks, while SPY’s intermediate TC is not Weak. It is a regime/strength sleeve — no Break and ReTest (BRT) zones — with a fixed multiplier stop/target and a long time stop.

1. What it is

Fill is always the next open after the trigger close. Gates are never re-checked on the entry bar. Role: multi-month leadership sleeve vs short-hold StockBee (SB) bursts and zone retest books (BRT / Year High / Pivot Break and Retest).

2. Entry logic

Signal bar T close — then fill at T+1 open. One position at a time per symbol.

  1. Require SPY_COMPARE_1Y, 2Y, and 3Y all > 0 (excess vs SPY). SPY.csv must be present.
  2. If rs_require_tc_strong=true (prod): stock IND_TC_*_OUTLOOK all Strong on T.
  3. If rs_spy_int_tc_not_weak=true (prod): lagged SPY TC (horizon int, lag 1) ≠ Weak (Strong or Neutral OK).
  4. Optional O'Neil-style filters (prod off): near-52-week-high, growth filter, Average True Range (ATR)% caps.
  5. Respect symbol_reentry_cooldown_days (prod 60) after a prior exit on that symbol.
  6. Fill at T+1 open. Do not re-check SPY_COMPARE / TC on the entry bar.

3. Exit logic

  • Stop: entry × stop_pct with stop_pct_is_multiplier=true → prod 0.85 (−15%).
  • Target: entry × target_pct → prod 1.25 (+25%).
  • Time stop: sell at close when bars held ≥ time_stop_days → prod 252 (set RS_TIME_STOP=0 to disable).
  • No-follow-through: no_ft_days=0 (off) in production.
  • sell_breakdown: off in production (research: breakdown_plus / only / both).
  • Gap / shared host exit schedule otherwise matches BRT Closed writers (no zone pivots).
Fidelity: stop and target can sit as broker orders. The 252-bar time stop is not a broker order — sell manually when due (watch Pending sells after DailyRun).

6. Caveats

  • Gates use trigger bar T only — entry open can gap against you with no TC re-check.
  • Requires indicator/TC + SPY series; use_indicators=true, indicator_buy=off.
  • min_spy_compare_1y_at_trigger and BRT too_high_multiplier are neutralized in run_rs.bat (would wrongly cut RS).
  • Freeze Closed still lists ATEYY historically; production universe/gate no longer include it.
  • Many A/B bats still default RS_STOP=0.88 — that is research BASE, not current prod.
