MTS — Magic Touch

MTS (Magic Touch) is the sheet-parity path against the STONK_DATA MTS tab (columns D:DP). It is not the Break and ReTest (BRT) retest pipeline: the buy gate is sheet BI on first touch after zone availability (DP), fill at next open.

1. What it is

Zones come from the sheet AF Touch Price stream (pivot-high and pivot-low), matured C14 = 7 bars later, then a 10-rung ladder overlap picks the active zone (DK:DN). Each zone episode fires one entry candidate on first touch (or when the active zone changes). There is no BY/DV retest-day COUNTIF (that is BRT/YH only).

2. Entry logic (sheet BI)

BI = AND of:

  1. BW Growth 3 Year: Close ≥ Close[756 bars ago].
  2. OR(BC, BC[-1]): Range Qualifier on this bar or prior (max high / min low over C24 − 1 > C7).
  3. BE: Close > Open.
  4. BG Level Acceptance: 7 closes above DK in the last 10 bars (needs AK).
  5. OR(AQ, AQ[-1]): Zone Eligible Long on this bar or prior.

When to evaluate: DP = first touch after availability (DO and (not DO[-1] or DN changed)). AW (AR≥C6 crossing) still counts touches but does not gate BC or create entries. Fill = next open after the first-touch bar.

Additional bat overlays on top of the preset: min_upper_wick_atr_at_trigger=0.25, min_dist_to_52w_high_pct_at_trigger=25, --symbol-reentry-cooldown-days 20. band_pct=0.018 is a manual override of optimizer 0.016.

3. Exit logic

  • Target: entry × target_pct → prod / sheet C3 path 1.22 (+22%).
  • Stop: signal-bar Low × stop_pct with stop_loss_based=trigger_low → 0.934 (sheet BJ / C4). Not entry-anchored.
  • Host gap / stop / target schedule.

6. Caveats

  • Do not describe MTS as “BRT with different numbers.” BI / DP first-touch is a different entry identity.
  • Preset disables many BRT gates (red-to-green, magic touch, spy-compare, too-high, …). Bat-level wick/52w gates are extra on top.
  • band_pct=0.018 is an explicit manual override — putting 0.016 back is a one-knob AB, not a silent restore.
