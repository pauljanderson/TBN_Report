WPBR — Pivot Break and Retest

WPBR (Pivot Break and Retest) builds weekly pivot zones, requires a weekly breakout plus confirmation, then buys a daily hold-above retest. It is not daily BRT and not the optional brt_like_wpbr research package.

1. What it is

Weekly pivot → zone band → Stage 1 weekly close above the upper band → Stage 2 weekly high confirmation (+3%) → daily retest from the Monday after the confirmation week → green daily close in a short window → next-open fill.

2. Entry logic

  1. Weekly pivot zones with strong-pivot either-mode: pre/post 3 bars / 10% (prod).
  2. Stage 1 breakout: first weekly close > zone_upper.
  3. Stage 2 confirm: first weekly high > zone_upper × (1 + wpbr_breakout_confirmation). Prod 0.03. Same week allowed.
  4. Daily retest starting the Monday after confirm week: stop_looking = first bar with Low ≤ upper AND Close > upper, but only before the first Close < zone_lower (sheet abandon-kill). No prior Close[r−1] ≥ lower gate. keep_looking is the legacy unbounded scan (A/B only).
  5. Entry window: wpbr_max_days_after_retest=2 (inclusive) for a green daily close above the upper band; fill = next open.
  6. Second chance after win: prod wpbr_second_chance_after_win=true.
  7. sheet_no_entry_same_bar_after_exit=false is forced (WPBR nosamebarexit off vs some sheet defaults).
  8. Growth filter off. SPY-compare floor neutralized (−1000). Indicators on for report columns, not as buy gates.

3. Exit logic

  • Stop: stop_pct=0.91 (wider than BRT’s 0.934).
  • Target: target_pct=1.22 (+22%).
  • Host gap / stop / target schedule. HALF_UP retest compares + variant C pivot rounding live in wpbr_zones.py.
