WRL — Weekly Range / Swing

Demand-zone bounce: previous-week range plus a walk-back swing high and swing low. Watch a daily close in the lower zone; buy the next day if price trades up out of it. Targets are the range high, then the swing high.

Research sleeve — not DailyRun-wired

How to run

Runner lives at the development root (same folder as DailyRun.bat): C:\Users\songg\Downloads\stockresearch\run_wrl.bat.

From that folder: run_wrl.bat → rocket_tbn.py -v wrl_mode=true → stock_analysis/rocket_wrl.py + stock_analysis/wrl_zones.py.

Default universe: Mag10 (AAPL,AMD,AMZN,AU,GOOGL,META,MSFT,NFLX,NVDA,TSLA) when drive/universes/WRL_universe.csv is missing. Override with run_wrl.bat ALL or set WRL_SYMBOLS=....

Target mode: set WRL_TARGET_MODE=scale (default, 50% at range high then remainder at swing high), range, or swing. Also -v wrl_target_mode=....

Outputs: drive/WRL_*_<ts>.csv (Closed / Open / Watchlist / Scanner / Summary / Audit / Equity) plus WRL_LatestRun_*.

Weekly levels

  1. Range high / range low — high and low of the previous completed week (week ending Friday strictly before the as-of daily bar). The in-progress week is never used.
  2. Swing high — walking backward from the week before that, the first weekly high that is higher than the previous week's high.
  3. Swing low — independently, the first weekly low that is lower than the previous week's low. Swing high and swing low may come from different weeks.

Valid structure requires swing_low < range_low ≤ range_high < swing_high.

Daily sequence

  1. If the daily close is inside the demand zone [swing_low, range_low] → WATCH.
  2. Next session: if the open is not through the swing low, and the high trades up out of the zone (High > range low) → BUY.
  3. Fill at the open if already above range low; otherwise at range low (intraday break).
  4. Stop at the swing low (or swing_low * stop_pct).
  5. Targets: range high (first) and swing high (stretch). Default scale takes half at range high, moves the stop to breakeven, and holds the rest for swing high.

Live artifacts

  • Watchlist / Scanner — last bar closed in the demand zone; next session is the breakout day.
  • Open — live position with frozen setup levels (range/swing from the watch week).
  • Closed — completed trades with both targets on the row (TARGET_PRICE / TARGET2_PRICE).

Do not

  • Treat this as a DailyRun gold sleeve until it has a freeze and a promotion bar.
  • Use the in-progress week's high/low as the range — only completed Friday weeks.
