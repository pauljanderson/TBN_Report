MOM — Momentum (Clenow)

MOM (Momentum) is a house research sleeve based on Andreas Clenow’s Stocks on the Move classic rules: weekly volatility-adjusted momentum ranking, 100-day Simple Moving Average (SMA) trend filter, SPY 200-day SMA regime gate, and Average True Range (ATR) risk sizing. It is a research candidate — not gold and not DailyRun-wired.

1. What it is

Long-only equity momentum: each week, rank liquid names by 90-day log-regression slope × R² (coefficient of determination), keep / buy the top ~20% of eligible names when the index is in an uptrend, size by ATR, and exit on rank or SMA failure. No classic hard stop.

2. Entry logic

  1. Universe: static liquid tradable tape (MOM_universe.csv = VZ 2010 / ADV$2m copy). Not point-in-time S&P 500.
  2. Trend: close above 100-day SMA.
  3. Gap filter: no single-day |move| > 15% in past 90 days (included; labeled).
  4. Rank: 90-day volatility-adjusted momentum; higher better.
  5. Pool: top 20% of eligible ranked names that review day.
  6. Index gate: new buys only if SPY > 200-day SMA.
  7. Size: shares ≈ (equity × 0.001) / 20-day Wilder ATR; fill from top of rank until cash used.
  8. Cadence: Wednesday review (signal at close → next open fill); re-size every other week.
