Production · Relative Strength Index (RSI)

RSI — Relative Strength Index

RSI (Relative Strength Index) is Wilder's 14-period heat gauge — how stretched a name is on its own tape — not RS (Relative Strength vs SPY). It buys a cool-off after a hot run, then sells when RSI is hot again, rolls off a peak, or the calendar clock hits. DailyRun sleeve — not walk-forward gold.

1. What it is

Hypothesis: after a name runs hot (RSI(14) ≥ 70), a pullback into a cooler band (still above oversold) on a lively tape is worth a short hold until heat returns, the move gives back, or time runs out. Role: short-hold heat-gauge sleeve vs multi-month RS (vs SPY) leadership and zone-retest books (Break and ReTest / Year High / Pivot Break and Retest / Volume Zone).

One position at a time per symbol. Live surface is Watchlist (RSI_Watchlist_<ts>.csv) — there is no RSI_Scanner file. Watchlist rows include what-if closes (price that would print RSI < 60 tomorrow, and the range needed for the ATR% floor).

2. Entry logic

Signal bar T close — then fill at T+1 open. Engine: _signal path in rocket_rsi.py (buy_base + gate_ok).

  1. Was hot: prior bar RSI(14) ≥ rsi_ob (house 70). This arms the setup; it is not the buy.
  2. Cool-off band: T close has 30 < RSI(14) < 70 (rsi_os=30, still below the overbought line). Below 30 is oversold here — not a buy.
  3. Trigger cap: T’s RSI must be < rsi_max_trigger (house 60). Combined with the band: house buy is prior ≥ 70, then 30 < RSI < 60 and trigger RSI < 60.
  4. Volatility floor: 14-day ATR / close × 100 ≥ rsi_min_atr_pct (house 2.93) at the trigger close. Quiet names never enter. This is not a stop.
  5. Optional DNA gates (house off): rsi_min_dist_to_52w_high_pct_at_trigger=0 (do not pass 7.18 — that wire was reverted), rel-vol min off, RSI-drop-from-overbought min off, near-high cap off.
  6. Fill: rsi_entry_on=next_open → Open of T+1. Alternate research: close of T. Do not buy T’s open using T’s close.
  7. One name at a time. symbol_reentry_cooldown_days=0 in house (off).

3. Exit logic

Evaluated on each bar’s close while in the trade; fills are the next open. Priority if more than one fires on the same bar:

  1. Roll-8 (RSI_ROLL): in-trade max RSI(14) − current RSI(14) ≥ rsi_roll_from_max (house 8) → sell next open.
  2. Overbought (OVERBOUGHT): RSI(14) ≥ rsi_exit (house 70) → sell next open.
  3. Clock (TIME): calendar days from entry ≥ rsi_time_stop_days (house 20) → sell next open.

6. Caveats

  • Not Relative Strength vs SPY. Prefix RSI_* is this sleeve; RS_* is the other.
  • No price stop and no dollar target. A gap through the next-open sell is the live risk.
  • Dummy $10k notional means Closed dollar columns are sheet-scale, not a 500k deployable book. Do not change DailyRun host sizing from this page.
  • Watchlist is the live surface. Convergence / reports that look for RSI_Scanner will show missing — that is expected.
  • Roll-8 and ATR% 2.93 are preference adopts, not walk-forward gold. OOS is report-only; do not retune to “fix” OOS.
  • Optional trigger DNA (7.18 min-dist, rel-vol, RSI-drop) stays off in house. Passing them silently is a different system.
  • Never pass entry_start_date / entry_end_date on production runs (research shop only).
  • run_rsi.bat ALL / research CSVs write their own stamps and must not overwrite RSI_house_last_run_ts.txt.
