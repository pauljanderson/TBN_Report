Not production gold. Not DailyRun-wired. Do not confuse with BRT (Break & Retest) production systems. VZ is a research prototype that builds zones from rolling max-volume days, then buys upside break → support retests. 1. What it is

VZ treats each rolling max-volume day (lookback window) as a persistent price zone — High–Low (HL) and/or Open–Close (OC). After price breaks up through the zone top, the system looks for a retest from above (touch / near-miss of the zone) within a retest window and buys that retest. Hypothesis: high-volume nodes act as support after acceptance above them.

2. Entry logic (predictive bar rules)

  1. Zone creation: each day after lookback_days, find the max-volume bar in the trailing window; when a new winner appears, create zone(s) that persist. Adopted freeze uses HL-only (zone_kinds=("HL",)).
  2. Break-up: close > zone.hi (with break_pct=0, break_atr=0 = no extra distance required), having been at/below the top sometime prior.
  3. Retest clock: within retest_window bars after break (adopted 63), scan for support retest from above.
  4. Signal bar T: bar intersects zone band (or near-miss within retest_eps_pct of zone.hi), approach from_above, close still ≥ zone.lo. Signal is known only at/after T’s close (needs T’s Low/High/Close).
  5. Quality gates (v2): first_retest_only=True; min_touches_before_entry ≥ 1.
  6. Fill (house default): entry_on=next_open → buy Open of T+1. Alternate: entry_on=close → buy Close of T (prior research AB freeze; still predictive). Forbidden: Open of T using T’s range.

3. Exit logic (adopted primary)

Exit recipe name: zone_atr05_ts40 (chosen in-sample on PaulTwenty exit compare — label selection bias).

Prior toy exit was zone.lo stop / 2R / 20d — superseded for research ABs by zone_atr05_ts40.

Fidelity: stop/target vs time exits. Zone stop and 2R target can sit in Fidelity. The 40-day time stop is not a broker order — sell manually when due.
