VZ — Volume Zone

VZ (Volume Zone) is a DailyRun official Twin Beacon Networks (TBN) sleeve: rolling max-volume days become High–Low (HL) price zones; after an upside break, the system buys a support retest. It is not Break and ReTest (BRT). TBN production sleeve / DailyRun official — not walk-forward gold.

1. What it is

Hypothesis: high-volume nodes act as support after acceptance above them. Default DailyRun universe is Paul78.142 (drive/universes/VZ_universe.csv).

2. Entry logic

  1. Zone creation: each day after lookback_days, find the max-volume bar in the trailing window; when a new winner appears, create zone(s) that persist. Adopted freeze uses HL-only.
  2. Break-up: close > zone.hi (break_pct=0, break_atr=0 = no extra distance), having been at/below the top sometime prior.
  3. Retest clock: within retest_window bars after break (adopted 63).
  4. Signal bar T: bar intersects the zone band (or near-miss within retest_eps_pct of zone.hi), approach from_above, close still ≥ zone.lo. Known only at/after T’s close.
  5. Quality gates (v2): first_retest_only=True; min_touches_before_entry ≥ 1.
  6. Volatility floor (atr4): 14-day Average True Range (ATR) must be ≥ 4% of trigger close (min_atr_pct_at_trigger=4.0 / VZ_MIN_ATR_PCT=4.0; min_atr_pct_at_entry=0). Quiet names fail and never enter. This is not a 4-ATR stop — it is a “is this name moving enough?” screen the scanner can apply on the trigger date (next-open fill is not known yet).
  7. Fill: entry_on=next_open → Open of T+1. Alternate research: close of T. Forbidden: Open of T using T’s range.

3. Exit logic

The house freeze name is EXIT_atr4_s025_r15_ts20 (PO adopt 2026-09-07; stop 0.25 chosen after ImprovePriority AB on the same 142 book — in-sample selection). Read the tokens left to right — atr4 is a volatility floor, not the stop:

  • atr4 — 14-day ATR must be ≥ 4% of trigger close (min_atr_pct_at_trigger / VZ_MIN_ATR_PCT=4.0). Quiet names fail this gate and never enter. Not a 4-ATR stop. The same atr4 prefix sat on the older EXIT_atr4_s05_r15_ts20 (0.5 ATR stop) — the 4 did not change when the stop did. House used entry-priced 4% until 2026-09-07; trigger-priced 4% is now the DailyRun identity (one gate only — entry gate off).
  • s025 — stop at zone low − 0.25×ATR (stop_atr_buffer=0.25).
  • r15 — 1.5R target (target_r=1.5): take profit at 1.5 times the entry-to-stop distance.
  • ts20 — 20-bar time stop (exit_bars=20).

House now measures that 4% ATR floor at trigger close (ATR14 / trigger_close ≥ 4%) so the scanner can decide on the signal date. The prior house used entry fill price (lookahead vs a live scanner). Quality A/B (vz_atr_trigger_vs_entry_ab_20260907) was HOLD (IS WR 60.5% vs 60.9%, AvgR 0.489 vs 0.535; OOS also slightly softer). Adopted as a product / scanner-live choice — not KEEP, not gold. Do not also apply the entry gate (that would be a tighter two-gate system).

6. Caveats

  • Universe: Paul78.142 is a user Paul Score list (likely IS-aware). 4-arm AB vs old house56 was HOLD on IS Ann ROR — product choice, not a process KEEP.
  • Stop 0.25: chosen after seeing ImprovePriority AB on the same 142 book → in-sample selection. Re-score IS/OOS under the adopted freeze; do not retune OOS.
  • House entry_on=next_open differs from prior AB freeze close — do not silently retune other freeze knobs on OOS.
  • ATR% at trigger: operational adopt after HOLD quality A/B (vz_atr_trigger_vs_entry_ab_20260907). Slightly worse IS/OOS quality vs entry-priced 4%. Adopted so the scanner can decide on the trigger date. Not KEEP. Not walk-forward gold. Do not retune the 4% floor on OOS.
  • Not walk-forward gold. DailyRun official ≠ gold promotion bar.
  • High Volume Node (HVN) overlap stays off.
  • One position per symbol (DailyRun lock 2026-09-15): do not buy a name we already hold. Always on in enrich_trade_rows. Older pyramid books (N=2708 / GOLD 20200506 doubles) are not the regression baseline — compare to VZ_LatestRun_Closed.csv / house pin.
