VZ — Volume Zone

VZ (Volume Zone) is a research prototype: rolling max-volume days become High–Low (HL) price zones; after an upside break, the system buys a support retest. It is not Break and ReTest (BRT) production and is not DailyRun-wired.

1. What it is

Hypothesis: high-volume nodes act as support after acceptance above them. Default research universe is DualPaul78 (drive/universes/VZ_universe.csv).

2. Entry logic

  1. Zone creation: each day after lookback_days, find the max-volume bar in the trailing window; when a new winner appears, create zone(s) that persist. Adopted freeze uses HL-only.
  2. Break-up: close > zone.hi (break_pct=0, break_atr=0 = no extra distance), having been at/below the top sometime prior.
  3. Retest clock: within retest_window bars after break (adopted 63).
  4. Signal bar T: bar intersects the zone band (or near-miss within retest_eps_pct of zone.hi), approach from_above, close still ≥ zone.lo. Known only at/after T’s close.
  5. Quality gates (v2): first_retest_only=True; min_touches_before_entry ≥ 1.
  6. Fill: entry_on=next_open → Open of T+1. Alternate research: close of T. Forbidden: Open of T using T’s range.
