BRT — Break and ReTest

BRT (Break and ReTest) is the core daily pivot-zone sleeve hosted by Twin Beacon Networks (TBN). TBN is the engine; BRT is one system prefix (BRT_*). It buys acceptance above a matured pivot band on a support retest.

1. What it is

Pivots create touches; overlapping touches inside a band_pct envelope mature a zone (default 2 touches). After maturity, a bullish candle (same day or next) arms a next-open entry, subject to tight-range, level-acceptance, growth, and sheet red-to-green gates.

2. Entry logic

Condensed from BRT_LOGIC_SPEC.md + run_brt.bat freeze:

  1. Pivots: local high/low in a ±k window with future confirmation (drop/rally). Sheet-touch Final Pivot High also requires post-drop and not-also-pivot-low. Consecutive same-price Final PHs are allowed (PO 2026-07-20).
  2. Zone band: touch price × (1 ± band_pct). Prod 0.0154. Overlapping bands are not merged.
  3. Maturity: touch count in lookback_long reaches touch_threshold (sheet 2).
  4. Strong-pivot filter: prod pre 7 bars / 8.1%, post 7 / 10.8% (bat sets the percents; spec default mode is pre).
  5. Tight range: (max high / min low − 1) over 105 bars must be > 35% or the level is blocked (dead-range qualifier).
  6. Level acceptance (7/10): at least 7 of the last 10 bars close above the anchored zone lower, tied to Support Test when enabled.
  7. Bullish candle window: Close > Open on the maturity bar or the next bar only. Fill = next session open after that candle.
  8. Sheet red-to-green: prod sheet_red_to_green_entry_enabled=true.
  9. Growth filter: prod on. brt_sheet_touch=true. min_spy_compare_1y_at_trigger=-1000 (neutralized). Cap filters 0 (disabled).

3. Exit logic

  1. Gap down: Open ≤ stop → GAP_DOWN @ open
  2. Gap up through target: Open ≥ target → GAP_UP @ open
  3. Intraday stop: Low ≤ stop → STOP_LOSS @ stop
  4. Intraday target: High ≥ target → TARGET @ target
  • Prod stop stop_pct=0.934 (multiplier vs trigger/entry-bar low per spec/host).
  • Prod target target_pct=1.21 (entry × 1.21). Always multiplier form — do not mix with percent-add form.
  • too_high_multiplier=0 (off).

6. Caveats

  • Spec stop-reference language (trigger-bar low vs entry-bar low) is in BRT_LOGIC_SPEC.md — production uses the host’s current stop_loss_based / multiplier path; do not mix percent-form stop_pct.
  • Sheet ladder (DE/DF/DG) is optional parity — see spec §3.5 and SHEET_PARITY_DIFF.md.
  • Related sleeves: WPBR (weekly pivots), YH (year-high origin), MTS (sheet BI first-touch, not BRT retest), IND (deprecated).
