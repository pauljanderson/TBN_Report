YH — Year High

YH (Year High) trades year-high / band setups on the Twin Beacon Networks (TBN) zone path with Break and ReTest (BRT) zones off. A new 52-week high becomes a candidate; the zone activates after a 3% move-away, then the book looks for breakout → support retest.

1. What it is

YH is a zone origin swap on the shared TBN host: zones come from rolling year-highs rather than BRT pivot-touch maturity. Once a YH zone is active, breakout / retest / fill / stop / target still ride the host’s zone path (not the Relative Strength or Rocket Launcher engines).

2. Entry logic

  1. New YH: High[t] > MAX(High[t − yh_lookback : t−1]) after warmup. Prod lookback 252 trading days.
  2. Activation (sheet memory): rounded High ≥ YH × (1 + yh_move_away_pct). Prod 0.03 (3% move away). yh_memory_mode=sheet matches the YH-tab state machine (active level + next candidate), not a single persisted cell.
  3. Zone band: YH price × (1 ± band_pct). Prod 0.015 (±1.5%).
  4. Breakout → retest → green entry on the host zone path (same family as BRT once the zone exists).
  5. Growth filter: prod growth_filter_enabled=true, growth_bars=756 (close ≥ close 3 years ago).
  6. Fill: next open after the signal bar (host convention).
  7. SPY-compare / ATR% / indicator-buy gates are neutralized in run_yh.bat (use_indicators=false, indicator_buy=off, spy/ATR bounds 0).

3. Exit logic

  • Stop: multiplier stop_pct=0.934 (stop_pct_is_multiplier=true).
  • Target: target_pct=1.21 (+21% vs entry).
  • Stop compare: stop_compare_round_decimals=-1 (full-float vs Low/High — YH-only; shared TBN default remains 2 decimals for BRT/WPBR/RL/RS). Fixes false STOPs such as AU 2025-02-28 (stop 28.44964 vs Low 28.45).
  • too_high_multiplier=0 (off). strong_pivot_mode=off.
  • Gap-down / gap-up / intraday stop / target — first match wins (host schedule).

6. Caveats

  • Do not enable BRT or WPBR zones on the same run — YH owns zone origin.
  • Sheet-mode activation uses rounded High vs full-precision Active YH Touch Price (AAPL 2014-10-22 / NVDA examples in the engine docstring).
  • Param-hint A/Bs froze stop/target/band — do not retune on out-of-sample (OOS) to “fix” a soft year.
