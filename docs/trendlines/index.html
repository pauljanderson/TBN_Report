What you asked 
is there a way you Can add oversold overbought, either numbers or indicators on the daily trend charts. And can you rerun it and then publish it, please?

In plain English: Add a daily “too hot / too cold” read on each chart (Relative Strength Index, RSI(14): ≥70 overbought, ≤30 oversold), show the latest number on the chart and in the table, then regenerate and publish the opens trendline pack.

Overlays
  • M / W / D trendlines — frozen fractal support (solid) & resistance (dashed) from segments.json / gen_trendlines_tos_studies.py (same geometry as ToS studies).
  • HV6m — gold box: max-Volume day High–Low in last 6 calendar months (from stamp), drawn from HV day through last bar.
  • VZ zone (house) — purple band: Vol Zone HL for the current rolling 126 trading-day max-volume winner (build_zones in tools/vol_zone_break_retest.py; house rocket_vz is HL-only). Light blue = same day's OC band for dual-zone context (not the house entry filter).
  • SMA20 / SMA50 / SMA100 — simple moving averages on daily close (pink / orange / brown).
  • RSI(14) — bottom panel Wilder RSI; red band ≥70 overbought, green band ≤30 oversold; last value annotated on the panel and listed in the table.
  • Nearest HL above / below — closest house Vol Zone HL band (126d history via build_zones) with zone low above last close (above) or zone high below last close (below). Dashed red/green guides on chart.
  • HV6m (calendar) and VZ HL (126 bars) often match but can differ on ties / window edges.
