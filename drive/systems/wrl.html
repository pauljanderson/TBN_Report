WRL — Weekly Range / Swing

Demand-zone bounce off last week’s range, enclosed by a walk-back swing high and swing low. Watch a daily close in the lower pocket; buy the next session if price trades up out of it. Targets are last week’s high, then the swing high.

1. What it is

Each daily bar is mapped to the previous completed Friday week (never the in-progress week). That week’s high and low are the range. Walking backward independently, the engine finds a swing high (first earlier week whose high is above the range high) and a swing low (first earlier week whose low is below the range low). Those two weeks can differ.

Hypothesis: when a daily close sits in the pocket between swing low and range low, the market is testing demand under last week’s range. If the next session opens without gapping through the swing low and then trades back up through the range low, buy that reclaim.

2. The four prices (weekly math)

  1. Previous week = last Friday week-end strictly before the as-of daily bar. Monday–Friday of the current week is ignored even if those days have already printed.
  2. Range high / range low = that previous week’s High and Low.
  3. Swing high = walk week-by-week backward from the week before the range week. Stop at the first week with High > range_high.
  4. Swing low = independent walk from the same starting point. Stop at the first week with Low < range_low.

Structure is valid only when swing_low < range_low ≤ range_high < swing_high. Otherwise that daily bar has no setup.

Worked numbers. Previous completed week High 105, Low 98. Swing high 110, swing low 95. Demand zone [95, 98].

  1. Monday close 97 — known at the close. WATCHLIST. No fill yet. You still do not know Tuesday’s high.
  2. Tuesday open 97.20 — held the swing low (not through 95), still under 98. Place a buy stop at 98 for Tuesday only.
  3. Tuesday, whenever price first trades 98 — that is the trigger and the buy. Engine fill = 98. (If the day later prints high 99.10, that only proves the stop would have been hit; you do not wait for 99.10.)
  4. If Tuesday never trades 98, cancel at the close. Watch dies. Do not leave the buy stop working Wednesday.

3. Live order (what to actually place)

The backtest sees a full daily bar, so it can ask “did High clear range low?” Live you cannot. The matching broker order is a buy stop (also called buy-stop or stop order to buy). In Fidelity: Buy, order type Stop, stop price = range low. It sits above the current price and becomes a market order when that price is traded.

Tuesday openWhat you doEngine fill
Open < swing low (e.g. 94)Do nothing. Gap through demand — watch dies.No trade
swing low ≤ Open < range low (e.g. 97.20)Rest a buy stop at range low (98) for that session only.98 if hit; else no trade
Open ≥ range low (e.g. 99)Already through. Buy the open (market / MOO). Not a stop.Open

Do not wait until price has already gone through 98 and then send a market buy — that is chasing, and you will pay worse than the engine’s 98 fill. The stop must be working at 98. A buy stop-limit (stop 98, limit 98) is the cautious cousin: you may miss the fill if it blasts through; the engine models stop-market at 98.

4. Daily sequence (engine)

  1. If already in a trade, and this is not the fill bar: manage exits. Then skip new entries.
  2. If yesterday was a WATCH: this bar is the only chance to buy that watch. Reject if Open < swing_low. Buy if High > range_low (backtest proxy for “the buy stop at range low was hit”). Otherwise the watch expires (a later close in the zone can re-arm).
  3. If flat: today’s close in [swing_low, range_low] inclusive → arm WATCH for tomorrow.

Fill = Open if already ≥ range_low, else range low (buy-stop fill). Skip if stop ≥ fill or primary target ≤ fill. Optional: skip unless structural reward/risk ≥ wrl_min_rr (default 0 = off).

5. Watchlist / Scanner / Open / Closed

  • WRL_Watchlist_* / WRL_Scanner_* — last bar closed in the demand zone; next session is the breakout day. Not a fill.
  • WRL_Open_* — live position; levels frozen from the watch week.
  • WRL_Closed_* — finished trades. TARGET_PRICE = T1, TARGET2_PRICE = swing high.

6. Exits

No exit management on the fill bar. Intraday order after that: gap through stop → remaining target at the open → stop → remaining target. Time stop default off.

ModeWhat happens
scale (default)50% at range high (TARGET1), stop to entry, remainder at swing high (TARGET2).
rangeFull size out at range high.
swingFull size out at swing high.

Default stop = swing_low * stop_pct with stop_pct=1.0.

7. How to run

From the development root (same folder as DailyRun.bat), on main:

run_wrl.bat — Mag10 if drive\universes\WRL_universe.csv is missing.

run_wrl.bat drive\universes\PaulTwenty_universe.csv — Paul Twenty.

run_wrl.bat ALL — full universe. set WRL_TARGET_MODE=scale|range|swing.

Research RR gate (off by default): set WRL_MIN_RR=2.

Outputs: drive/WRL_*_<ts>.csv plus WRL_LatestRun_*.

8. Do not

  • Use this week’s high/low as the range — only a completed Friday week strictly before the as-of bar.
  • Buy the watch-day open. Fill is the next session only.
  • Keep a watch alive for more than one session. Cancel the buy stop at the close if it was not hit.
  • Wait for the day’s high to print, then market-buy — that is chasing. The buy stop must be working at the range low.
  • Buy a gap that opens below the swing low, even if the high later reclaims the range low.
  • Wire this into DailyRun gold or the $500k mix until there is a freeze and a promotion bar.
