WRL — Weekly Range / Swing

Demand-zone bounce off last week’s range, enclosed by a walk-back swing high and swing low. Watch a daily close in the lower pocket; buy the next session if price trades up out of it. Targets are last week’s high, then the swing high.

Not production gold. Not a DailyRun numbered step. Not in the $500k allocation mix. Do not treat Watchlist rows as same-day fills. The last bar closing in the zone only arms tomorrow’s breakout check.

1. What it is

Each daily bar is mapped to the previous completed Friday week (never the in-progress week). That week’s high and low are the range. Walking backward independently, the engine finds a swing high (first earlier week whose high is above the range high) and a swing low (first earlier week whose low is below the range low). Those two weeks can differ.

Hypothesis: when a daily close sits in the pocket between swing low and range low, the market is testing demand under last week’s range. If the next session opens without gapping through the swing low and then trades back up through the range low, buy that reclaim. First target is last week’s high; stretch target is the swing high.

Runner run_wrl.bat → rocket_tbn.py -v wrl_mode=true → stock_analysis/rocket_wrl.py + stock_analysis/wrl_zones.py. Weekly bars come from the same W-FRI resample as WPBR (wpbr_zones.aggregate_weekly).

2. The four prices (weekly math)

As of any daily bar dated D:

  1. Previous week = last Friday week-end strictly before D. Monday–Friday of the current week is ignored even if those days have already printed.
  2. Range high / range low = that previous week’s High and Low.
  3. Swing high = walk week-by-week backward from the week before the range week. Stop at the first week with High > range_high. That week’s High is the swing high.
  4. Swing low = independent walk from the same starting point. Stop at the first week with Low < range_low. That week’s Low is the swing low.

Structure is valid only when swing_low < range_low ≤ range_high < swing_high. If either walk fails, or the four prices are not nested that way, that daily bar has no setup (no watch, no buy).

price ▲ │ ──────── swing high (earlier week High > last week’s High) │ │ ──────── range high = previous completed week High ← T1 (scale / range mode) │ last week’s range │ ──────── range low = previous completed week Low ← breakout line (buy trigger) │ demand zone [swing_low, range_low] │ ──────── swing low (earlier week Low < last week’s Low) ← stop ▼

Worked numbers

Suppose the previous completed week (ended Friday) printed High 105, Low 98.

  • Walk back: first earlier week with High > 105 printed 110 → swing high = 110.
  • Walk back: first earlier week with Low < 98 printed 95 → swing low = 95.
  • Those two weeks need not be the same week.

Demand zone = [95, 98]. Targets = 105 then 110. Stop = 95 (default stop_pct=1.0).

Monday close 97 → WATCH (97 is inside [95, 98]).

Tuesday open 97.20, high 99.10 → BUY (open is not through 95; high cleared 98). Fill = 98 (open was still under the range low).

3. Daily sequence (exact engine rules)

One symbol, one position at a time. Each bar is processed in this order:

  1. If already in a trade, and this is not the fill bar: manage exits (see §5). Then skip new entries.
  2. If yesterday was a WATCH: this bar is the only chance to buy that watch.
    • Reject if Open < swing_low (gap down through the swing — watch dies).
    • Buy if High > range_low (traded up out of the zone).
    • Otherwise the watch expires. A later close in the zone can re-arm a new watch.
  3. If flat: if today’s close is in [swing_low, range_low] inclusive → arm WATCH for tomorrow. Uses this bar’s completed-week levels (they can change on Monday when a new Friday week locks in).
Predictive timing. Watch is known at the close of day T (needs T’s Close). Fill is on day T+1 only. The engine never buys the open of the watch morning using that morning’s range.

4. What Watchlist / Scanner mean

After the backtest finishes, if the last daily bar closed in the demand zone and there is no open position, the symbol is written to Watchlist and Scanner. That is not a fill. It means: next session, buy if the open holds the swing low and the high trades through the range low.

FileMeaning
WRL_Watchlist_*Last bar closed in [swing_low, range_low]. Next session is the breakout day.
WRL_Scanner_*Same names, with suggested stop / T1 / T2 and a one-line hint.
WRL_Open_*Live position. Levels are frozen from the watch week (not re-computed each day).
WRL_Closed_*Finished trades. TARGET_PRICE = T1, TARGET2_PRICE = swing high.

5. Exits

No exit management on the fill bar (same house rule as MVCP). Stops and targets start on the next session.

Intraday order on a management bar:

  1. Gap through the stop at the open → GAP_DOWN (or STOP_LOSS if open prints exactly on the stop).
  2. Else gap through the remaining target at the open → GAP_UP / scale labels below.
  3. Else low tags the stop → STOP_LOSS at the stop price.
  4. Else high tags the remaining target → fill at that target.
  5. Optional time stop (default off): close remaining at the close after N bars held.

Target modes (wrl_target_mode)

ModeWhat happens
scale (default) 50% at range high (TARGET1), stop moved to entry (breakeven), remainder at swing high (TARGET2). If the open gaps through both, the row’s average exit is the weighted fill.
range Full size out at range high. Label TARGET (or GAP_UP if the open is already there).
swing Full size out at swing high. Same labels. T1 is set equal to T2 so getTarget / Open show one number.

Default stop = swing_low * stop_pct with stop_pct=1.0 (stop sits on the swing low). After a scale T1 hit, the working stop becomes the entry price; the Closed row still stores the original swing-low stop in STOP_PRICE.

8. Live targets (getTarget)

getTarget.py system WRL (aliases RANGE, SWING, WEEKLY_RANGE) freezes range high / swing high / swing low from the completed-week structure as of the entry date. It does not re-walk weeks after you are in the trade.

9. Do not

  • Use this week’s high/low as the range — only a completed Friday week strictly before the as-of bar.
  • Walk swing high vs the adjacent week’s high. The walk is vs range high / range low, independently.
  • Buy the watch-day open. Fill is the next session only.
  • Keep a watch alive for more than one session. No breakout tomorrow → watch dies.
  • Buy a gap that opens below the swing low, even if the high later reclaims the range low.
  • Wire this into DailyRun gold or the $500k mix until there is a freeze and a promotion bar.
